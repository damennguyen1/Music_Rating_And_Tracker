1. Problem and intended user

Our application is for people who want to keep track of music they listen to.
Users can manually add songs and albums, rate them, and organize their favorites.

2. three main tasks

Task 1: Add and Rate Music

Users enter a song or album title, artist and rating from 1-10.
The program saves the entry and displays its information.

Users can search for songs by name or artists.
the program displays matching results and allows users to sort by rating or alphabetically.

Task 3: Top Five Favorites

Users select and rank their top five artist, songs, and albums.
The program displays three separate lists that users can update.

3. Preliminary Classes

Songs Stores title, artist, and rating.
Album stores album title, artist, and rating
MusicLibrary: Manages adding, searching, and organizing music.
Favorites manages the three top five lists. Maybe by genre as well??
File Manager Saves and loads user data.

4:Saving and Error Handling 

The program saves music entries, ratings, and favorites to a local file.
Invalid ratings outside 1-10 will display an error without changing saved information.
Missing or corrupted files will dispay an error without deleting existiong valid data.

5:Scope and exluded features

No music streaming or playback.
No Spotify or Apple Music integration.
No online accounts or sharing.
